GPR means Gaussian Process Regression. All eight kernels include white-noise observation variance, selected with length scale on 2025 data. Regression scores are sigmoid-calibrated into win probabilities; this is not Gaussian process classification. Noise does not randomly flip picks. Market-input models use spreads and totals and are not directly equivalent to no-market models.

Training and evaluation details

Each GPR fit uses up to 1,000 recent regression games and a separate, later calibration block of at least 256 games. The final calibration block includes completed 2026 games. The displayed evaluation was measured before that final fit. Missing current features retain historical fallbacks. Kernel uncertainty is distinct from the assumed noise used in season simulations.

New team metrics and sources

The 16 expanded candidates use eight estimator families with two feature views: team differences or full matchup context. They train on 2017 onward, tune on 2025, and evaluate on 2026 before final fitting. GPR candidates also include the new team differences. The original eight retain their earlier feature definitions.

Early-down EPA
Mean Expected Points Added on first and second down; offensive production and defensive EPA allowed.
Sacks, QB hits, third downs
Sacks and recorded hits per dropback; third-down conversions per third-down play. Hits are not a complete pressure metric.
Red-zone efficiency and run stuffs
Touchdowns per drive reaching the opponent's 20-yard line; rushes gaining zero or fewer yards per rush.
Next Gen Stats
Time to throw, intended air yards, tight-window throws, completion percentage over expected, rushing yards over expected, receiver separation, and yards after catch over expected. Team rates weight qualifying players by attempts or targets.
Team strength
Custom Elo ratings with offseason regression, plus the average pregame Elo of prior opponents. Not an official NFL rating.

Performance metrics use only earlier weeks, averaged over the last five team games. Public PBP/NGS history begins in 2016 for this feature set. NGS excludes players below reporting thresholds; missing values are imputed from training data, not filled with future results. Historical data are revised snapshots, not timestamped original releases.

Approximate movement in the model output caused by a feature.

Home team value minus away team value. Positive means the home team has the edge.
EPA
Expected Points Added. Higher offensive EPA is better; lower defensive EPA allowed is better.
Rolling windows
Last 3 or last 5 games before a matchup.

Spreads and betting totals are excluded so future schedule predictions do not depend on betting lines.

Expected Scores, Spreads & Totals

Expected points come from a separate Random Forest / Extra Trees regression ensemble, not the winner classifier. Decimal scores are averages, not exact-score forecasts, and can imply a different winner. Lines are nflverse snapshots, not live prices. Missing lines are not estimated.
